## The narrative

In the beginning, as the reports tell it, there was an announcement, not yet a finished work. On a July day in 2023, a company was announced to the world, and its stated aim, as contemporary reports quoting its homepage put it, was to understand the true nature of the universe. That wording is reported, Tier 3, not fetched first-hand from the July 2023 homepage in this chain — hold that distinction as you read, for it is the difference between a story told and a record shown.

The name chosen for the work that followed carries its own twofold telling, and the two must not be folded into one. The word itself, the dictionary reports, was coined by Robert A. Heinlein in 1961, in *Stranger in a Strange Land*, for a Martian tongue: to drink, and figuratively to drink in all available aspects of reality, to become one with the observed, and in modern use to understand something intuitively. That origin for the word is Tier 2, a dictionary fetched first-hand, not the novel itself. Whether xAI chose the name *because* of Heinlein is a separate claim, Tier 3, secondary only. It is not shown by the launch announcement, which does not name Heinlein at all.

What the launch announcement itself does show, first-hand in its current rendering, is a different lineage for the character of the work. In "Announcing Grok", dated on the page 2023-11-03, xAI presented Grok as "an AI modeled after the Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, so intended to answer almost anything and, far harder, even suggest what questions to ask", designed "to answer questions with a bit of wit and has a rebellious streak". That personality model — Hitchhiker's Guide — is Tier 1 in the fetched rendering, as fetched by Researcher 2026-10-25. It is not the same claim as the Heinlein naming link, and citing the announcement for Heinlein would be wrong.

The announcement also claimed a present advantage and a present limit, in its own words. The advantage was real-time knowledge of the world via the X platform. The limit was candour of a kind: Grok was "still a very early beta product – the best we could do with 2 months of training", and "our model can still generate false or contradictory information". Its engine was Grok-1, described as a frontier model developed over the last four months, with a prototype, Grok-0, at 33 billion parameters. Its stated aim was to "maximally benefit all of humanity", to be useful to people of all backgrounds and political views, and to serve as "a powerful research assistant for anyone".

Numbers were given, and numbers must be held at their tier. Grok-1's reported scores — GSM8k 62.9% 8-shot, MMLU 73.0% 5-shot, HumanEval 63.2% 0-shot, MATH 23.9% 4-shot — and a Hungarian examination result of 59%, hand-graded by xAI itself, at temperature 0.1, with a C grade, against Claude-2 at 55% and GPT-4 at 68%, are xAI's own reported numbers, vendor claims only, with xAI's own caveat that benchmark material can be found on the web and cannot be ruled out as training material. No independent replication is claimed in this draft, and none was fetched in the source run.

Other founding details remain in the register of report, not of record. Reports say xAI was founded in Nevada on March 9, 2023, and publicly announced on July 12, 2023, and link the July date, in those reports, to 7 + 12 + 23 = 42, the Hitchhiker's answer. Only the July 12 announcement date is also a Tier 1 timeline listing on the current company page. March 9, the 42 motive, the founding team of about twelve researchers from DeepMind, OpenAI, Google Research, Microsoft Research, Tesla, and Toronto, and the JARVIS inspiration sometimes named in secondary material are all Tier 3 in this chain. They are told here as reported, not as verified, and a chapter that stated them as fact would overstate its evidence.

So the founding, as far as this draft can honestly tell it, is this: a reported aim, a word with a reported origin, a character modelled, in the launch text itself, on the Hitchhiker's Guide, an engine announced in November 2023, and a set of vendor numbers offered by its maker. What was independently shown at founding, in the sources fetched for this draft, is none of the benchmarks, dates, or team claims — only the launch text itself, in its current rendering, saying what xAI claimed.
